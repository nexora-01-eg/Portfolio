FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a referral to book an appointment?

No referral is required for most departments. For specialty follow-up care such as neurology or advanced orthopedic procedures, our care team will coordinate any necessary referrals on your behalf.

Which insurance plans do you accept?

We're in-network with BlueCross Horizon, Aetna Signature, Cigna Premier, UnitedHealth Pinnacle, Humana Elite and MetLife Assured. We also offer transparent self-pay pricing for uninsured patients.

How fast can I get an appointment?

Most new patients are seen within 72 hours, and existing patients typically within 24–48 hours. Same-day urgent slots are held daily for pressing concerns.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Please bring a photo ID, your insurance card, a list of current medications, and any recent lab or imaging results from previous providers if available.

Can I message my doctor between visits?

Yes — every patient gets access to our secure patient portal for messaging, prescription refill requests, and reviewing lab results directly with their care team.

Do you offer telehealth visits?

Telehealth is available for follow-ups, medication management, and select consultations across most departments — simply request a virtual visit when booking.

What is your cancellation policy?

We ask for at least 24 hours' notice for cancellations or reschedules so we can offer the slot to another patient. Late cancellations may incur a small administrative fee.
