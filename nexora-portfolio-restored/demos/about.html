Care that remembers you're a person first.

Meridian Health was founded on a simple idea: medicine works best when there's time for it. Here's our story, our values, and the team behind every visit.

Our story

Built by a doctor who was tired of six-minute appointments.

After ten years practicing internal medicine inside a large hospital system, Dr. Elena Marsh kept running into the same wall: brilliant clinicians, impossible schedules, and patients who left more confused than when they arrived.

In 2008 she opened Meridian Health with three exam rooms and one promise — every patient gets the time their case actually deserves. Eighteen years later, that promise now extends across eight departments and 24 specialists, but the exam rooms still run 45 minutes, not 10.
